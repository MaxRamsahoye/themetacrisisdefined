    The Metacrisis

    The Metacrisis is (a conceptual frame for capturing) civilisation’s total problem complex, the generative dynamics that (re)produce it and the attractor states it vectors towards. It marks the terminal phase in the developmental process of the current life-world-system, which is now emergently posing an existential risk to humanity. It is a historical period in which the future of civilisation will be determined: exhibiting concurrent tendencies of collapse and catastrophe, dystopia and suffering, protopia and flourishing. The metacrisis results from a fundamental orientation (separate, rivalrous, instrumentalising) operationalised in dominant institutional structures (nation-states, market-capitalism, corporations) and enacted in the dominant second nature of minds (self-preservation, resource-acquisition, power-seeking).
